English Reading Practice

Наведи курсор на слово, чтобы увидеть перевод. Нажми на слово, чтобы услышать произношение.

My name is Kate. I am a smart girl. I am not lazy.

This is my friend Sam. He is tall and calm. He is not short. His hair is dark and his T-shirt is green.

This is Mary. She is happy today. She is not sad. She has a gray cat. The cat is on a chair next to the stairs.

We are in a school. We are good friends. We are not in the park. Our classroom is not big.

Look at Tom and Rose. They are in the yard. They are not in the house. Tom has a black book and Rose has a blue notebook.

You are my teacher. You are fair and kind. You are not angry.

It is a hot day. It is not cold. There are clouds in the sky, but there is no storm.

A brown bird is in the garden. A green frog is next to the pond. A gray mouse is under a tree. The bird, the frog, and the mouse are not afraid.

At the afternoon we watch a film about a king and a queen. The king is brave. The queen is kind. They are not poor. Their room is golden and big.
